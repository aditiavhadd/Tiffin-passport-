Why this is different

The prototype addresses the practical failure points we found in the market.

01 — Area-first

Service coverage is local. Users choose city/region and neighbourhood before seeing recommendations.

02 — Compare apples-to-apples

Price, portion, spice, menu rotation, delivery and subscription flexibility are shown in one structure.

03 — Trial before commitment

A one-meal trial reduces the risk of paying for a monthly plan before knowing the actual experience.

Prototype data uses publicly visible/current Mumbai-area service listings as examples. Prices, menus, delivery areas and availability can change and should be verified with the provider before an actual order.
